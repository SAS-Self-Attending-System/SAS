# SAS--Self Attending System

Hello there is the offical GitHub page for Self Attending System.

The Self-Attending System is a thoughtfully developed digital platform designed to modernize and simplify the daily attendance process within a school environment, providing students with a convenient and efficient way to record their attendance while ensuring that every attendance request is properly verified and securely maintained. By reducing dependence on traditional manual procedures, the system helps save valuable time, and create a more organized approach to attendance management. At the same time, it provides teachers and authorized administrators with a clear and reliable platform through which attendance can be reviewed, verified, updated, and monitored, including the management of regular and afternoon attendance as well as on-duty records. Through its combination of simplicity, security, accuracy, and ease of use, the Self-Attending System aims to create a smoother and more responsible attendance experience for students, teachers, and the school as a whole.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


1. What is the Self-Attending System?


The Self-Attending System is a digital school attendance platform designed to simplify attendance management. It allows students to submit attendance requests, teachers to verify and manage attendance records, and authorized administrators to maintain the system efficiently.


2. How do I access the website?


Open the official website and click the LOGIN button. You will be directed to the login interface to access the appropriate portal.


3. Do students need a password to log in?


Yes. The student login is designed to work a password. Students select their assigned roll number and name to access the student portal.


4. How do I mark my attendance?


Log in to the student portal, select Mark Attendance, choose Morning or Afternoon Attendance, and submit your request. Your attendance will be updated after a teacher reviews and approves the request.


5. Is my attendance marked immediately?


No. Submitting a request does not automatically mark you Present. A teacher must approve your request before the attendance record is updated.


6. What happens if attendance is closed?

If a teacher has closed the relevant attendance period, students cannot submit new attendance requests for that period until it is reopened.


7. What does P, A, or — mean?


- P — Present: Your attendance has been marked Present.


- A — Absent: Your attendance has been marked Absent.


— — Not Marked: No attendance status has been recorded yet.


8. What is the On-Duty feature?


The On-Duty feature allows an eligible student to submit an on-duty request through the student portal. The system checks the student's attendance status before allowing the request.

9. Can students change their own attendance records?


No. Students can submit attendance requests, but attendance changes must be made by an teacher.


10. What can teachers do?


Teachers can view student attendance, approve or reject attendance requests, edit attendance records, open or close attendance periods, reset attendance when authorized, and manage on-duty records.


11. What should I do if I cannot log in?


Check that you are using the correct account details and that your internet connection is working. If the problem continues, contact the teacher or the developers.


12. What should I do if my attendance is incorrect?
 

Contact your teacher and explain the issue. An a teacher can review the record and make corrections.
